That only happens if something bridges the UCG's LAN ports to the SR1041F's LAN (most likely a cable from the SR1041F — old LAN3 cable — still plugged into the floor switch as a second uplink, or another link such as the old second TP-Link, MAC 28:f7:d6:ac:79:31, at 192.168.100.121 answering as 192.168.0.1 with its own DHCP). Result: three DHCP servers/gateways on one segment; devices holding 192.168.0.x whose gateway 192.168.0.1 resolves to the old second TP-Link have NO working IPv4 internet (Parts Control PC and Terminal 1 on Wi-Fi: 1.1.1.1:443 fails, google -4 times out) but IPv6 via the SR1041F works — so IPv6-capable sites load and IPv4-only ones like global.canon (no AAAA record) fail. Verified on Parts Control PC with curl -4 vs -6. Fix (physical, awaiting Danial): find and remove the cable joining the SR1041F side to the floor switch / UCG side (only SR1041F LAN1 -> UCG WAN should link them), then power off the old second TP-Link, then renew DHCP on affected PCs.
